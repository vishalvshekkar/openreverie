# Code of conduct

This project exists to help people through hard parts of their lives. The community around it holds itself to the same care.

## Expectations

- Be kind and assume good faith. Many people here may be dealing with more than they mention.
- Never mock, dismiss, or speculate about anyone's mental health, in issues, reviews, or anywhere else in project spaces.
- Treat any accidentally shared personal data as radioactive: do not quote it, do not screenshot it, report it to the maintainer so it can be scrubbed.
- Disagree about code plainly and respectfully. Critique the work, never the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, or sustained hostility of any kind.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported privately to vishalvshekkar@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences scale from a private conversation to permanent removal from project spaces, at the maintainer's judgment.
